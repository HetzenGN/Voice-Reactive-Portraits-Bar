Voice Reactive Portraits Bar Companion Installed

The browser companion is ready. No Developer Mode or DevTools are required.

  1. Open your Foundry VTT world with Voice Reactive Portraits Bar enabled.
  2. While viewing that Foundry tab, click the Voice Reactive Portraits Bar Companion browser extension.
  3. Click Pair Current Foundry Tab.
  4. In the Voice Reactive Portraits Bar Relay Controller, click Open StreamKit.
  5. Configure your Discord user mappings in Reactive Portrait Configuration.

Open Voice Reactive Portraits Bar documentation on GitHub
